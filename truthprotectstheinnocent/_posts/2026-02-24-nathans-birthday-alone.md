## The Context

Nathan is an Australian father who has been separated from his partner Catherine and their three children — Utopia, Galorian, and Blue Bell — since late 2025. The children were removed by Italian authorities based on three official justifications. **All three have since been disproven by Italian authorities themselves:**

- ✅ Children were vaccinated — confirmed by the Assembly of Chieti and Teramo
- ✅ Education was legal — confirmed by the Ministry of Education
- ✅ Home was safe — confirmed by a certified structural surveyor

The twins turn 7 on March 4th. Nathan's birthday has come and gone without his family. There is no legitimate legal basis for this separation.
