The Story

Why We're Gathering

A family's story that represents a bigger truth about parental rights worldwide

#FamigliaNelBosco

"Family in the Woods"

In Italy, a family made a choice that many of us dream about: to raise their three children close to nature, away from screens, with home-based education focused on real-world learning.

They weren't neglectful. They weren't abusive. They were different.

And for that difference, their three children were taken and placed in a facility. The mother can visit—but she cannot stay. She cannot hold her children through the night. She could not be with them on Christmas morning. She will not be with them when they wake on New Year's Day.

💔 The Daily Reality

  • Their father can only see his own children for one hour per week
  • Children raised in nature, without screens, with early bedtimes and whole foods—are now in a facility with TVs, late nights, and processed food—a complete reversal of everything they knew

💔 The Double Standard

Ask yourself: Why are children removed from loving parents who choose homeschooling and nature—while children in homes with alcoholism, violence, and true neglect remain?

  • Different is not dangerous
  • Alternative is not abusive
  • Natural living is not neglect
  • Homeschooling is not harm

This could happen to ANY family that doesn't conform. Today it's them. Tomorrow it could be you.

🌍 The Bigger Picture

This family's story is not unique. Around the world, families face similar situations when their lifestyle choices differ from mainstream expectations—even when there is no abuse, no neglect, only love expressed differently.

We gather not to attack or blame, but to shine light. When thousands of people focus their attention on a situation, things that were hidden often come to light. Decisions that were made in shadow are reconsidered in daylight.
