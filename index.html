What Happened

From Idyllic Life to State Seizure

A timeline of how a family living peacefully in the Italian countryside became the center of a global human rights concern.

July 28, 2022

The Family Settled

Catherine Birmingham and Nathan Trevallion moved to the Italian countryside, a peaceful home where they were raising their children naturally.

September 9, 2022

The 60 Minutes Report

60 Minutes Australia aired an episode about their natural, unconventional lifestyle. The family was portrayed as an oddity. A Pandora's box was opened.

October 5, 2022

The Seizure

Armed carabinieri arrived at the family's home. The children were taken. No arrest. No conviction. The state came for the children, not the parents.

Today
