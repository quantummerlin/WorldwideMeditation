The Story

Why the Meditation Began

A brief overview of the events that led to this community gathering. Based on publicly available media reports. Please verify independently.

July 2022

Family Relocated to Italy

Catherine Birmingham and Nathan Trevallion moved to rural Italy, where they were raising their children in a natural, low-tech environment. The family was homeschooling and living close to nature.

September 2022

Media Coverage

A television program aired a segment about the family's lifestyle. The program described their unconventional approach to education, technology, and living. Following this, questions about the family's circumstances were raised in Italian media.

October 2022

Children Placed in State Care

Italian authorities placed the children in the care of child welfare services. The parents were not arrested or convicted of any crime. The placement was made under Italian child welfare procedures, which the family has been challenging through legal channels.

2023 – Present

Community Response

Community members organized meditations, awareness campaigns, and petitions in support of family reunification. The case continues to be discussed in Italian and international media. The meditations have become a regular gathering for people who believe in the power of collective intention.

⚖️ Important: This is a community awareness page. It is not affiliated with any legal team representing the family. All information is based on publicly available media reports. Facts should be independently verified. This page has not been reviewed by a lawyer. Respect the ongoing legal process in Italy.
